Chapter 1: Introduction to the Windmill Third-Person View / 3D World Map
Once upon a time there lived an old man farmer named Manoy in a farmhouse with his kid named Rene. Rene is playing around without understanding safety rules. Old Man Manoy warned him: "Never be fond of playing within the windmill! Once you push the button in the wrong place, you will be blown away by the legendary wind!" Manoy leaves to buy a pack of corn for breakfast. Will Rene obey, or will he push the buttons?
